# Recipe Summary

## Tomato Pasta

Category: Dinner
Time: 20 minutes
Servings: 2

### Required Ingredients

- pasta
- canned tomatoes
- onion
- garlic clove
- parmesan cheese
- dried basil
- cooking oil
- salt
- black pepper

### Instructions

1. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions.
2. Chop the onion and garlic.
3. Fry the onion and garlic in cooking oil.
4. Add the canned tomatoes and dried basil.
5. Simmer the sauce for 10 minutes.
6. Season with salt and black pepper.
7. Combine the sauce with the pasta.
8. Serve with parmesan cheese.

## Cheese Omelette

Category: Breakfast
Time: 10 minutes
Servings: 1

### Required Ingredients

- egg
- cheese
- milk
- butter
- salt
- black pepper

### Instructions

1. Crack the eggs into a bowl.
2. Add the milk, salt and black pepper.
3. Whisk until combined.
4. Melt the butter in a frying pan.
5. Pour the egg mixture into the pan.
6. Add the cheese when the eggs begin to set.
7. Fold the omelette and cook until the cheese has melted.

## Chicken Curry

Category: Dinner
Time: 35 minutes
Servings: 4

### Required Ingredients

- chicken breast
- onion
- garlic clove
- coconut milk
- curry powder
- rice
- cooking oil
- salt

### Instructions

1. Cook the rice according to the package instructions.
2. Cut the chicken into small pieces.
3. Chop the onion and garlic.
4. Fry the onion, garlic and chicken in cooking oil.
5. Add the curry powder and coconut milk.
6. Simmer for 15 minutes.
7. Season with salt and serve with rice.

## Carbonara

Category: Dinner
Time: 25 minutes
Servings: 2

### Required Ingredients

- spaghetti
- egg
- bacon
- parmesan cheese
- black pepper

### Instructions

1. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions.
2. Fry the bacon until crisp.
3. Mix the eggs and parmesan cheese in a bowl.
4. Drain the spaghetti and combine it with the bacon.
5. Remove the pan from the heat and add the egg mixture.
6. Stir quickly and season with black pepper.

## Pancakes

Category: Breakfast
Time: 20 minutes
Servings: 4

### Required Ingredients

- egg
- milk
- flour
- butter
- salt

### Instructions

1. Mix the flour and salt in a bowl.
2. Add half of the milk and whisk until smooth.
3. Add the remaining milk and the eggs.
4. Melt the butter and mix it into the batter.
5. Fry thin pancakes in a hot frying pan.

## Pasta Bolognese

Category: Dinner
Time: 45 minutes
Servings: 4

### Required Ingredients

- ground beef
- onion
- garlic clove
- olive oil
- beef bouillon cube
- red wine
- canned crushed tomatoes
- tomato paste
- sugar
- worcestershire sauce
- bay leaf
- salt
- black pepper
- spaghetti
- parmesan cheese

### Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat.
2. Add the onion and garlic and cook for 3 minutes until soft.
3. Increase the heat and add the ground beef.
4. Cook the beef until browned, breaking it apart with a spoon.
5. Add the red wine and simmer for 1 minute.
6. Crumble in the bouillon cube and stir.
7. Add the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, sugar, Worcestershire sauce and bay leaf.
8. Season with salt and black pepper.
9. Bring to a simmer, then reduce the heat and cook for 25 minutes, stirring occasionally.
10. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions.
11. Drain the spaghetti and remove the bay leaf from the sauce.
12. Combine the spaghetti with the sauce.
13. Serve with grated parmesan cheese.

## Vegetarian Lasagne with Pesto and Mozzarella Cheese

Category: Dinner
Time: 75 minutes
Servings: 6

### Required Ingredients

- olive oil
- onion
- garlic clove
- mushrooms
- zucchini
- eggplant
- canned crushed tomatoes
- sugar
- salt
- black pepper
- ricotta cheese
- parmesan cheese
- lasagne sheet
- basil pesto
- mozzarella cheese

### Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 200 degrees Celsius.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat.
3. Add the onion and cook for 3 minutes until soft.
4. Add the garlic, mushrooms, zucchini and eggplant.
5. Cook for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables are golden.
6. Add the crushed tomatoes and sugar.
7. Season with salt and black pepper.
8. Simmer for 10 minutes.
9. Mix the ricotta and parmesan cheese in a bowl.
10. Spread a thin layer of the vegetable sauce in a baking dish.
11. Add a layer of lasagne sheets.
12. Spread a layer of the ricotta mixture, a spoonful of pesto and a layer of the vegetable sauce.
13. Sprinkle with mozzarella cheese.
14. Repeat the layers until the ingredients are used, finishing with sauce and mozzarella.
15. Cover the dish with foil and bake for 30 minutes.
16. Remove the foil and bake for 15 minutes until golden and bubbling.
17. Let the lasagne rest for 10 minutes before serving.
