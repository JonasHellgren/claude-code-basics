# Recipe Summary

## Carbonara

Category: Dinner
Time: 25 minutes
Servings: 2

### Required Ingredients

- pasta
- egg
- bacon
- parmesan cheese
- black pepper

### Instructions

1. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions.
2. Fry the bacon until crisp.
3. Mix the eggs and parmesan cheese in a bowl.
4. Drain the spaghetti and combine it with the bacon.
5. Remove the pan from the heat and add the egg mixture.
6. Stir quickly and season with black pepper.

## Chicken Curry

Category: Dinner
Time: 35 minutes
Servings: 4

### Required Ingredients

- chicken breast
- onion
- garlic
- coconut milk
- curry powder
- rice
- cooking oil
- salt

### Instructions

1. Cook the rice according to the package instructions.
2. Cut the chicken into small pieces.
3. Chop the onion and garlic.
4. Fry the onion, garlic and chicken in cooking oil.
5. Add the curry powder and coconut milk.
6. Simmer for 15 minutes.
7. Season with salt and serve with rice.

## Cheese Omelette

Category: Breakfast
Time: 10 minutes
Servings: 1

### Required Ingredients

- egg
- cheese
- milk
- butter
- salt
- black pepper

### Instructions

1. Crack the eggs into a bowl.
2. Add the milk, salt and black pepper.
3. Whisk until combined.
4. Melt the butter in a frying pan.
5. Pour the egg mixture into the pan.
6. Add the cheese when the eggs begin to set.
7. Fold the omelette and cook until the cheese has melted.

## Pancakes

Category: Breakfast
Time: 20 minutes
Servings: 4

### Required Ingredients

- egg
- milk
- flour
- butter
- salt

### Instructions

1. Mix the flour and salt in a bowl.
2. Add half of the milk and whisk until smooth.
3. Add the remaining milk and the eggs.
4. Melt the butter and mix it into the batter.
5. Fry thin pancakes in a hot frying pan.

## Tomato Pasta

Category: Dinner
Time: 20 minutes
Servings: 2

### Required Ingredients

- pasta
- tomato
- onion
- garlic
- parmesan cheese
- basil
- cooking oil
- salt
- black pepper

### Instructions

1. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions.
2. Chop the onion and garlic.
3. Fry the onion and garlic in cooking oil.
4. Add the canned tomatoes and dried basil.
5. Simmer the sauce for 10 minutes.
6. Season with salt and black pepper.
7. Combine the sauce with the pasta.
8. Serve with parmesan cheese.
