# Pancakes

Category: Breakfast
Time: 20 minutes
Servings: 4

## Ingredients

* 2 eggs
* 250 ml milk
* 150 g flour
* 25 g butter
* 1 pinch salt

## Instructions

1. Mix the flour and salt in a bowl.
2. Add half of the milk and whisk until smooth.
3. Add the remaining milk and the eggs.
4. Melt the butter and mix it into the batter.
5. Fry thin pancakes in a hot frying pan.
