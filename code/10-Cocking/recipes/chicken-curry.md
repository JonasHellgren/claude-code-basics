# Chicken Curry

Category: Dinner
Time: 35 minutes
Servings: 4

## Ingredients

* 500 g chicken breast
* 1 onion
* 2 garlic cloves
* 400 ml coconut milk
* 2 tablespoons curry powder
* 250 g rice
* Cooking oil
* Salt

## Instructions

1. Cook the rice according to the package instructions.
2. Cut the chicken into small pieces.
3. Chop the onion and garlic.
4. Fry the onion, garlic and chicken in cooking oil.
5. Add the curry powder and coconut milk.
6. Simmer for 15 minutes.
7. Season with salt and serve with rice.
