# Tomato Pasta

Category: Dinner
Time: 20 minutes
Servings: 2

## Ingredients

* 200 g pasta
* 400 g canned tomatoes
* 1 onion
* 2 garlic cloves
* 50 g parmesan cheese
* 1 teaspoon dried basil
* Cooking oil
* Salt
* Black pepper

## Instructions

1. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions.
2. Chop the onion and garlic.
3. Fry the onion and garlic in cooking oil.
4. Add the canned tomatoes and dried basil.
5. Simmer the sauce for 10 minutes.
6. Season with salt and black pepper.
7. Combine the sauce with the pasta.
8. Serve with parmesan cheese.
