# Pasta Bolognese

Category: Dinner
Time: 25 minutes
Servings: 4

## Ingredients

* 500 g ground beef
* 1 onion, chopped
* 2 garlic cloves, minced
* 2 tablespoons olive oil
* 1 beef bouillon cube
* 100 ml red wine
* 800 g canned crushed tomatoes
* 1 tablespoon tomato paste
* 1 teaspoon sugar
* 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
* 1 bay leaf
* Salt
* Black pepper
* 400 g spaghetti
* Parmesan cheese

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat.
2. Add the onion and garlic and cook for 3 minutes until soft.
3. Increase the heat and add the ground beef.
4. Cook the beef until browned, breaking it apart with a spoon.
5. Add the red wine and simmer for 1 minute.
6. Crumble in the bouillon cube and stir.
7. Add the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, sugar, Worcestershire sauce and bay leaf.
8. Season with salt and black pepper.
9. Bring to a simmer, then reduce the heat and cook for 25 minutes, stirring occasionally.
10. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions.
11. Drain the spaghetti and remove the bay leaf from the sauce.
12. Combine the spaghetti with the sauce.
13. Serve with grated parmesan cheese.
