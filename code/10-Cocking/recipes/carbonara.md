# Carbonara

Category: Dinner
Time: 25 minutes
Servings: 2

## Ingredients

- 200 g spaghetti
- 2 eggs
- 100 g bacon
- 50 g parmesan cheese
- Black pepper

## Instructions

1. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions.
2. Fry the bacon until crisp.
3. Mix the eggs and parmesan cheese in a bowl.
4. Drain the spaghetti and combine it with the bacon.
5. Remove the pan from the heat and add the egg mixture.
6. Stir quickly and season with black pepper.
