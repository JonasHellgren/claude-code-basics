# Book Search Criteria

* **Topic:** Reinforcement learning
* **Minimum publication year:** 2025
* **Level:** Beginner
* **Language:** English
* **Publisher:** Major international publisher
* **Coding language:** No specifik
* **Maximum number of books:** 3


## Selection Requirements

* The book must primarily focus on reinforcement learning.
* The publication year must be 2025 or later.
* The book must be suitable for beginners.
* The book must be available in English.
* Prefer recently published books with practical examples.
* Exclude books with insufficient or unreliable publication information.
* Major international publisher. For example, springer, CRC press
* No specific coding language
