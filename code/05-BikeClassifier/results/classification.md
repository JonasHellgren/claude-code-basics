|---|---:|
| Model | Example Bike |

| Engine power | 32 kW |

## Result

Classification: A2

## Explanation

- The motorcycle does not satisfy the A1 rules: engine volume (400 cm³) exceeds the A1 limit of 125 cm³.
- Engine power (32 kW) does not exceed the A2 limit of 35 kW.
- Power-to-weight ratio (0.188 kW/kg) does not exceed the A2 limit of 0.2 kW/kg.
- All A2 conditions are satisfied, so the motorcycle is classified as A2.
