# Motorcycle Licence Rules

## A1

Classify the motorcycle as `A1` when all conditions are satisfied:

* Engine volume is at most 125 cm³.
* Engine power is at most 11 kW.
* Power-to-weight ratio is at most 0.1 kW/kg.

## A2

Classify the motorcycle as `A2` when all conditions are satisfied:

* Engine power is at most 35 kW.
* Power-to-weight ratio is at most 0.2 kW/kg.

## A

Classify the motorcycle as `A` when it does not satisfy all conditions for `A1` or `A2`.

## Calculation

Calculate the power-to-weight ratio as:

`engine power in kW / weight in kg`

## Classification order

1. Check `A1`.
2. If the motorcycle does not satisfy all A1 conditions, check `A2`.
3. If it does not satisfy all A2 conditions, classify it as `A`.
